ABOUT GETESTIMATEFAST

Helping people start local service projects with clearer information

GetEstimateFast is a connection and marketplace platform focused on helping people describe service needs and connect with independent local professionals in Tampa Bay and nearby communities.

What GetEstimateFast does

Instead of calling multiple companies one by one, customers can describe a project once, provide useful details, and submit a request through the platform. GetEstimateFast reviews and organizes requests so relevant independent professionals can understand the project and decide whether they want to respond.

Service availability depends on the project type, location, timing, and the professionals currently using the platform.

What GetEstimateFast does not do

GetEstimateFast does not perform, supervise, or guarantee the services requested through the platform. Independent professionals are responsible for their own estimates, qualifications, licenses, insurance, permits, contracts, workmanship, pricing, schedules, and customer agreements.

Customers should evaluate professionals and verify any requirements that apply to the specific type of work before hiring.

For customers

Customers can use GetEstimateFast to start requests for home improvement, repairs, maintenance, cleaning, remodeling, roofing, plumbing, electrical, HVAC, painting, flooring, drywall, handyman work, and other supported service categories.

The goal is to make the first step clearer: one structured request, better project context, and a simpler way to compare available options.

For professionals

Independent service professionals can create accounts, build public profiles, describe their service categories and coverage areas, and discover potential project opportunities that match their business.

A GetEstimateFast profile or account is not a certification or endorsement. Professionals remain responsible for the accuracy of their business information and for complying with applicable licensing, insurance, advertising, consumer-protection, and trade requirements.

Where we focus

GetEstimateFast currently focuses on Tampa Bay, Florida, with local service pages and request flows for Riverview, Brandon, Valrico, Apollo Beach, Gibsonton, and other nearby communities as the platform expands.

Transparency matters

Submitting a request does not guarantee that a professional will respond, provide an estimate, accept a project, or perform work. GetEstimateFast is designed to help organize the connection process while leaving hiring decisions and project agreements directly between customers and independent professionals.
