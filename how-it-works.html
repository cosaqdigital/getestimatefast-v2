CLEAR EXPECTATIONS

What GetEstimateFast does — and does not do.

We organize the connection

We provide the platform for customers to describe service needs and for eligible professionals to discover relevant opportunities.

Professionals are independent

Service providers are not GetEstimateFast employees, agents or representatives.

Responses are not guaranteed

Submitting a request does not guarantee an estimate, response, hire, price or outcome.

You make the hiring decision

Customers and professionals agree directly on scope, pricing, scheduling and any project contract.

FAQ

Common questions about how it works.

Is GetEstimateFast free for customers?

Customers can submit a service request without paying GetEstimateFast to create the request.

Does submitting a request guarantee an estimate?

No. Availability and responses depend on the service, location and participating professionals.

Who may contact me after I submit a request?

Eligible independent professionals who serve your area and receive access under platform rules may contact you.

Does GetEstimateFast perform the service?

No. GetEstimateFast is a marketplace platform and does not perform or supervise the requested services.

How does GetEstimateFast work for professionals?

Professional accounts are designed to organize business details, service categories and service areas, with marketplace tools opening in stages as they are approved.
