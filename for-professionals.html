FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers about professional accounts, opportunities and the rollout.

Is a GetEstimateFast professional profile free?

Yes. Creating and maintaining your self-service professional profile is free. Optional paid marketplace features or services are separate and should be shown clearly before purchase.

Does GetEstimateFast guarantee jobs?

No. Opportunity availability depends on local demand, your service categories, your coverage area and participating customers.

Can I choose where I want to work?

Yes. Professional accounts are designed to let businesses define their service categories, base ZIP Code and service radius.

Will I know the price before buying a customer contact?

When paid marketplace access is enabled, applicable contact pricing and terms are intended to be shown before a purchase decision.

Can I have a public business profile?

Yes. Professional profiles can be published through GetEstimateFast when the required profile information and applicable account requirements are complete.
