Frequently asked questions

Is the professional account free?

Yes. Creating an account and building a business profile is free. If optional paid features launch, their prices will be shown before purchase.

Will I receive guaranteed jobs?

No. Requests depend on demand, your service categories and ZIP Code. GetEstimateFast does not guarantee leads or contracts.

Can I have a public page for my work?

The Development workspace contains a public-profile feature. You control what is published once this feature is approved for launch.
