Privacy Notice for Requests from Professional Profiles

When you request an estimate from a public professional profile, GetEstimateFast collects the name, email, phone number, ZIP Code, city, service category and project description you provide.

Who can receive the request

Your request is attributed to the featured professional whose profile you visited. After a GetEstimateFast review, that professional may receive the contact details without charge. The request may also appear in a marketplace where other local professionals can purchase authorized access to the same contact details. This request is not exclusive to the featured professional. Submitted contact information is not displayed in the public opportunity listing.

How we use the information

We use it to review your project, coordinate potential estimates, record which professional's page generated the request, prevent abuse, and administer the marketplace. A service request is not a guarantee of a quote or hire. Consent to communications about the submitted project is separate from consent to marketing text messages; submitting this form does not authorize marketing SMS.

Profile owners and visitors

A professional's private registration phone/email is not displayed on their public page through this request flow. A professional can share a public page and invite eligible customers to leave reviews. Reviews are subject to moderation.

Your choices

You can choose not to submit a request. To ask questions about personal information, correct a submission or ask about deletion, contact admin@getestimatefast.com. Some records may need to be retained for operational, legal or security purposes.

This notice covers requests originating from public professional profiles in GetEstimateFast's independent US platform. It does not create any connection with another platform.
