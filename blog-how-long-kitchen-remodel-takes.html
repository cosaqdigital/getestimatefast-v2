How Long Does a Kitchen Remodel Take?

A kitchen remodel can range from a quick surface update to a multi-stage renovation that takes several weeks or longer. Cabinets, layout changes, countertop fabrication, plumbing, electrical work, permits, and material lead times usually matter more than square footage alone.

The project scope determines the kitchen timeline

Painting walls, replacing hardware, or updating a backsplash is very different from removing cabinets, changing plumbing locations, installing new flooring, rewiring lighting, and building an island.

A full kitchen renovation commonly takes multiple weeks of active construction, and planning or product lead times can add more time before work begins.

Typical phases of a kitchen remodel

Projects may move through design and selections, demolition, rough plumbing and electrical work, wall or floor repairs, cabinet installation, countertop measurement and fabrication, backsplash, fixtures, appliances, and final trim.

Some phases depend on the previous one being complete. For example, countertop templates are often taken after cabinets are installed and confirmed.

  • Planning, measurements, and product selections
  • Demolition and structural or wall preparation
  • Plumbing and electrical work when required
  • Cabinet installation and countertop templating
  • Backsplash, flooring, fixtures, appliances, and finish work

The most common schedule delays

Cabinet lead times, special-order appliances, countertop fabrication, changes after demolition, hidden damage, permit or inspection timing, and late design decisions can all extend a schedule.

Homeowners can reduce some delays by finalizing the layout early, confirming appliance specifications, and making sure critical materials are ordered before demolition.

Can you live in the house during the remodel?

Many homeowners stay in the home, but a full kitchen remodel can temporarily remove normal cooking, sink, and food-preparation access. Ask when water, power, appliances, and the kitchen sink will be unavailable.

A temporary food-preparation area elsewhere in the home can make longer projects easier to manage.

What to include when requesting kitchen estimates

Share whether you are keeping the existing layout, replacing cabinets, changing countertops, moving plumbing, adding an island, replacing flooring, or updating lighting and appliances. Photos and rough measurements help clarify the starting point.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a full kitchen remodel usually take?

A full remodel commonly takes multiple weeks of active construction, with additional planning and material lead time depending on the scope and products selected.

What causes kitchen remodel delays?

Cabinet or appliance lead times, countertop fabrication, hidden conditions, change orders, permit timing, and late selections are common causes.

Do countertops get installed before or after cabinets?

In many projects cabinets are installed and leveled before final countertop measurements are taken, but the contractor and fabricator determine the sequence.
